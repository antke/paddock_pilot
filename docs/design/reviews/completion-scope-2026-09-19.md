## Finite completion criteria

Keep three separate dimensions rather than treating all remaining limits as unaudited UI:

1. **Audit reviewed:** source ownership, applicable meaningful local states, actual composition rendering, keyboard/responsive observations, identified defects, fixes and appropriate checks are recorded. A separately assembled substitute cannot close a unique controller/composition gap.
2. **Integration verified or explicitly unverified:** real authentication, backend persistence/permissions, external providers and route transitions retain their own evidence or boundary. A locally audited actual view is not falsely called untouched solely because this dimension remains open.
3. **Follow-up limits:** physical-device/AT, print output, uncommon variants and other unperformed validation are explicit, with concrete material failures distinguished from optional depth. Shared owners can receive representative checks; each route need not repeat every control permutation.
