**Yes—the controls need clearer grouping and more explicit action labels.** The established typography, palette and record cards work well; the toolbar still behaves like a desktop calendar wrapped onto a phone.

The source and live interface confirm three different jobs: navigating dates, narrowing records, and creating training entries. They currently share almost identical button treatments and several disconnected rows.

1. **P1 — The creation labels obscure their different purposes.** “Add training session” starts scheduled work; “Add training log” starts completed work. Rename the first to **Plan session**, position it as a quiet text action beside the page title, and retain your exact **Add training log** label in the fixed bottom bar. The filled button earns its prominence through frequent completed-work entry and thumb access. Impeccable lens: clarify.
2. **P2 — Unrelated controls are grouped together.** Week/Month shares a row with creation, while Filters occupies another row. Put date navigation in one row; put a joined Week/Month selector and Filters in the next. Use quiet chevrons for previous/next, pale selection for the active period, and a labeled filter control with its active count. All retain at least 44px touch targets. Lenses: layout, quieter.
3. **P2 — Controls postpone the actual records.** At 390 × 844, the first record starts around y474; roughly 180px separates the period heading from the agenda. Remove the mobile explanatory paragraph and combine the range heading with date navigation. Keep the page title and start records directly after the two compact control rows. Lens: distill.
4. **P2 — Expanded filters make returning to results awkward.** Horse choices extend beyond the initial usable viewport. Keep active-filter summaries and Clear, and add **Show results** at the end of the panel to collapse it and bring the agenda into view. Preserve Escape and focus return. A bottom sheet can follow if this still proves cumbersome; it is unnecessary for the first refinement. Lens: adapt.
